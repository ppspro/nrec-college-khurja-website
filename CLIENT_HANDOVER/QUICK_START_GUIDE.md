# NREC College CMS — Quick Start Guide

Get your college website updated in under 10 minutes!

---

## Step 1: Log In

Go to: **http://your-domain.ac.in/admin/login**

- **Email:** admin@nreccollege.ac.in
- **Password:** admin@123
- Click **"Log In to Admin Panel"**

---

## Step 2: Explore the Dashboard

After login, you'll see the Dashboard with:
- **Quick Stats** (total news, events, courses)
- **Recent Activity** (what was last changed)
- **Quick Actions** (add news, post notice instantly)

---

## Step 3: Do Your First Task

### Post a Notice (2 minutes)
1. Click **Campus Life → Notice Board**
2. Click **"Add Notice"**
3. Enter Title and Content
4. Click **"Save"**
5. Open the website — your notice is live!

### Add a Faculty Member (3 minutes)
1. Click **Academics → Faculty**
2. Click **"Add New Faculty"**
3. Fill in name, designation, department
4. Upload a photo
5. Set to **Active** and **Save**

### Upload a Gallery Photo (2 minutes)
1. Click **Campus Life → Gallery**
2. Select or create an album
3. Click **"Upload Images"** and choose photos
4. **Save** — photos appear instantly on the website

## Quick Tips

✅ **Always verify** changes by opening the website in a new tab  
✅ **Save as Draft** to prepare content in advance  
✅ **Compress images** before uploading (use squoosh.app — free)  
✅ **Logout** when done, especially on shared computers
